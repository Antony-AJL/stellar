Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription? ›
Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Find Stellar and tap Cancel Subscription. Your access continues until the end of the billing period. We don't manage cancellations directly — Apple handles all subscription billing.
Can I get a refund? ›
Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, find your Stellar purchase, and request a refund. Apple reviews all refund requests.
How do I delete my account? ›
Open Stellar → tap the Profile tab → Delete Account. This immediately and permanently deletes all your data from our servers. This cannot be undone.
Can Stellar read my journal entries? ›
No. Your knowledge graph is encrypted on your device before it reaches our servers. We don't hold your encryption key. Your journal entries are sent to Google Gemini for AI processing, but the raw text is not stored by us — only the resulting encrypted graph is kept.
I lost access to my account. Can you recover my data? ›
If you signed in with Apple, sign in again on the same Apple ID. If you used email/password, use the forgot password flow. Because your data is encrypted with a device-derived key, data recovery across devices may not be possible if your device was lost without a backup.
The app isn't working — what should I do? ›
Try force-quitting and reopening the app. If the problem persists, contact us via our form with your device model, iOS version, and a description of what happened. We'll get back to you within 2 business days.
Why does voice journalling need microphone access? ›
Voice input uses Apple's on-device Speech Recognition to transcribe what you say. Audio is processed on your device — we never receive audio recordings.
